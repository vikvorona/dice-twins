Frequently Asked Questions

How do I earn coins?
You earn coins by completing levels. Higher difficulty levels reward more coins. You can also watch optional ads to double your reward after a win.
What are coins used for?
Spend coins on Undo moves (5 coins) or Suggest hints (20 coins), and to unlock dice skins in the Shop.
How does the merge mechanic work?
Move a die so its top face matches the top face of an adjacent die of the same type — they'll merge! Later levels introduce color and shape dice with triple-merge mechanics.
My progress was lost. What should I do?
Progress is saved locally on your device. If you deleted and re-installed the app, progress cannot be restored. We're working on cloud saves for a future update.
How do I turn off ads?
Ads are optional rewarded ads only — you're never forced to watch one. Interstitial ads appear occasionally between levels as you play.
The game is frozen / crashing. What can I try?
Try force-quitting and reopening the app. If the problem persists, please email us with your device model and iOS version.
