Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 19, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Dice Twins ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the Dice Twins mobile application ("App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect or store any personally identifiable information. The App stores game progress (completed levels, coins, settings) locally on your device only.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including optional rewarded video ads and interstitial ads. AdMob may collect and use data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. This may include:

  • Device identifiers (advertising ID)
  • IP address and general location (country/region)
  • App usage data and interactions with ads
  • Device type, OS version, and language

Google's use of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → turn off Personalized Ads
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

3. Data Storage

All game progress (level completions, coin balance, purchased skins) is stored locally on your device using the platform's file storage. This data is not transmitted to any server by us. If you uninstall the App, this data is permanently deleted.

4. Children's Privacy

Dice Twins is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

5. Third-Party Links

The App does not contain links to third-party websites or services beyond advertising provided by AdMob.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
